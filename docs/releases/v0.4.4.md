## love-mini 0.4.4

### Install

With Port Store 1.3.0 or later it updates by itself. By hand: unzip **love-mini-0.4.4.zip** to
the root of the SD card.

### Fixed

- Balatro's table was white. With reduced motion, Balatro draws its background once into a
  picture with a rectangle and the background shader. Shapes drawn with a shader that never
  reads its texture are now that shader's colour, as sprites drawn with it already were.
- Textures no longer go round in circles over the budget. Before, a picture drawn two frames
  ago could be dropped and loaded from its file again at once, a pause of up to a second
  every few seconds in Balatro. Now what was drawn in the last 90 frames stays, and the
  scaled copies of pictures go before the pictures themselves.
- No scaled copy is made of a picture when the copy would be bigger than the screen. Those are
  atlases, drawn a part at a time: a copy of all of it took more memory than it saved time.

### New

- `cpu_mhz` in a profile: the CPU's clock while the game runs, set with OnionOS's `cpuclock`
  and put back after. Balatro's profile asks for 1500.
- The log says where a frame's time goes, every 10 seconds: the game's update and draw,
  sleeping, how long mini2d drew (blits, fills, shapes, clearing, textures) and which
  love.graphics calls a frame makes. When textures pass the budget, it says what they were.
